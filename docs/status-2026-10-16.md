## What works

- **1,117 deals from 12 schools**, each with its source and date. Explore defaults to NTU (157 student deals);
  a student of another school picks theirs once. Search covers names, place (臺北市大安區…), English names and
  九折-style discounts. Expired deals are hidden (still reachable from Saved).
- **Google sign-in** (needs your OAuth client, below) records email, domain and login days. Full terms,
  contacts and links unlock after sign-in; saved deals also work offline.
- **使用優惠 pass**: 15-minute QR + one-time code, live clock, "show your student ID too".
- **Shops without an app**: create a shop link in `/admin.html`, open it once on the counter phone, then scan
  passes with the phone camera. The cashier screen shows the deal, its terms, who it is for, the student's
  masked Google account and 「請核對學生證後再確認」. Only shop-confirmed passes count as verified.
- **School cards (CampusPass 校園卡)**: one account holds several school identities, each verified on its own
  (school Google account; test login for now), shown as wallet-style cards on /me with a self-declared role and a
  one-year expiry. Explore defaults to 我的學校 (all your cards' schools); /me sources are grouped by them; the
  cashier sees the student's matching verified card. Verifying by a code to the school email needs an email
  provider (your choice) and shows as 即將推出.
- **Merchant leads** at `/merchants`; **delete account** on /me.
- **Founder dashboard** `/admin.html` (paste ADMIN_TOKEN, which campass-2 gave you): the go/no-go gate
  (students using deals twice in 30 days vs 30; shops that would pay vs 5), users, redemption evidence by tier,
  CSVs, shop links.
- **PWA**: installable on iPhone/Android, offline after one visit, safe areas, light/dark, 中文/English.

## Known limits

- Tested in Chromium only; iOS Safari/standalone behaviour is emulated, not run on WebKit.
- Google sign-in doesn't prove enrolment; the shop still checks the student ID (the business plan's rule).
- Some sources are stale or vague: 臺大系統學生會 last updated 2022 (flagged on each deal); 26 中興 deals don't
  state who qualifies (shown as staff-only, noted); hotel rates in PDF attachments weren't read.
- On iPhone, a shop that pairs from a home-screen icon must type codes (scanning opens Safari); the shop page
  says so.
- After a data deploy, browsers may see the previous deal list for up to 5 minutes (cache).
- **Test login is off again** (removed at your request, ~05:00 UTC). Until the Google credentials are set,
  nobody can sign in: tapping 登入 returns to the same page with 「Google 登入即將開放」. Browsing works. The
  test accounts were deleted (your OK), so production starts from 0 users.
- **Always Use HTTPS** is on (thank you): plain http now redirects everywhere.
- The tecxmate.com zone auto-injects Cloudflare Web Analytics; our CSP blocks it (2 console errors per page,
  no effect on the app). Recommended: exclude pass.tecxmate.com from Web Analytics in the Cloudflare dashboard.
