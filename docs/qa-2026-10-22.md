# Demo QA round, 2026-10-22

Four parallel testers on a local build (phone, tablet/desktop, pass and shop flows, language/accessibility)
plus campass-2's sweep of production. **No P0 anywhere.** No console errors, no horizontal page scroll (except
the cashier screen, fixed below), axe-core clean on 14 screens, zh/en dictionaries in step. The screenshots
were in the session's scratchpad and are not kept.

## Fixed in this round

- **English mode:** discount badges are in English (9折 → "10% off", 85折起 → "15%+ off", 折$100 → "NT$100 off";
  all 87 badge texts in the data have a rule), and English search finds them. The area shows the city
  in English ("Taipei"). Source names stay as written (data).
- **Bad areas in the data:** the importer read 茶門市, 西區市, 義門市臺北市, 臺北市臺北市, 高雄市前鎮 and others. It now
  requires a real city or county and prefers a district ending in 區 (14 deals corrected, nothing else
  changed). **Production needs `npm run db:seed:remote`** for these to show.
- **Toasts** use the full width (`width:max-content`): no more two-line toasts at half the screen.
- **Phone filter row:** under 430px the count shows the number only (the full text stays for screen
  readers), and it covers the row's right padding, so no pill peeks out half-hidden.
- **Cashier screen** (/r and /m, English) fits a 390px phone: shorter labels ("Google account", "Test
  login"), tags can't overflow, and 「測試」 is no longer said twice on the card line. Blank lines in clamped
  terms are collapsed (no lone 「…」 line). After 確認 on the scan page, the page returns to the top.
- **Shop page:** a second tap of 確認使用 with an empty box keeps 「已確認使用」. 「本店的優惠」 instead of the
  uppercased brand. iPhone guidance no longer contradicts itself (Safari: bookmark it and scan; home screen:
  type the code).
- **Pass:** once expired, it no longer says 「請同時出示學生證」 or "show the cashier".
- **Admin stats:** the "most viewed" and "most passes" tables leave out test (dev-login) accounts, like the
  tier counts, so the page no longer contradicts itself.
- **Copy:** 校園卡 used consistently in Chinese (身分 only for the self-declared role); the duplicate disclaimer at
  the foot of a deal is gone; the missing space in the English add-card sheet; the delete-account sentence;
  the /me privacy note now lists everything stored (favourites and deal uses too); "Not for" goes
  through i18n; the /merchants heading no longer wraps at 360 in English.
- **Touch:** the main hover styles (rows, stars, icon buttons, pills, outline/ghost buttons, menu options,
  card buttons) only apply on devices that hover, so nothing stays highlighted after a tap.
- **Small layout:** 404 shows the brand in the app bar on phones; the desktop detail header shows the
  title as soon as the name scrolls under it; the Appearance switch drops its icons below 380px; the role
  picker on school cards is 40px tall on touch screens.

## Not reproduced

  back at 2,500px, locally and on production (e3dc6c3), at 390. Ask for the exact steps if it shows up again.
- **Sign-in button on /m and /r** (campass-2): not shown on the shop pages at 390 or 1280, with a valid or an
  invalid key. Only `/r/` with no code (a 404) shows the normal app bar.

## Open (not fixed; ranked)

1. **Shop confirm window vs pass:** a shop can confirm for 30 minutes (`CONFIRM_MINUTES`, src/index.ts) while
   the student's pass expires after 15, so the cashier can confirm a pass the student sees as expired.
   docs/business-review.md says 2 hours. Pick one rule.
2. **A shop can confirm a pass the student marked 沒有使用** (the reverse is blocked with 409): contradictory
   evidence in the redemptions CSV.
3. **"Did it work?"** is asked on every close, even 2 seconds after opening, and again for a reused pass.
   Ask once per code, and only after the pass was shown for a while.
4. **Shop links can't be revoked** (no endpoint or admin button), though README and the business review say so.
5. **Desktop:** the school picker (641px tall) covers its own pill at 1280×800; a keyboard-focused category
   pill stays under the › arrow; focus rings clipped by `overflow-x:auto` on `.filterrow`/`.pills`; Escape on a
   deal opened by direct link drops focus to body; hover looks like selected; at 1280+ the list jumps from
   centred to left when the detail opens; /me language/theme switches stretch ~590px.
6. **Tablet 768:** empty /saved and /nope scroll 104px; the pass's time box stretches to 728px.
7. **All schools (1,096 rows) at 4× CPU:** 1.1s fill with 50–240ms long tasks; the scrollbar thumb jumps.
8. **Copy and meta:** English page titles mix "CampusPass — campus deals" and "X · CampusPass"; index.html
   meta/og stay Chinese; offline.html ignores the language; the shop manifest's description is the student
   app's; admin says 通行證 where the app says 憑證; zh 。 at the end of toast vs error strings is inconsistent;
   unused keys (app.name, search.shortcut, detail.address, detail.reportHint).
9. **Data:** some offers have flattened price tables, e.g. 承億文旅 (da6ff66207) 「$1,400$1,600$2,200藍天雅緻雙人房…」.
   Avoid it in the demo, or fix the text in the importer.
10. **No pass history** for the student after a confirmation or an answer.
11. **/me at 360:** a normal school email is cut ("r12345678@nt…") because Sign out takes the room.
12. Font preloads warn "preloaded but not used" on direct /deal/<id> loads (the phone detail covers the wordmark).
